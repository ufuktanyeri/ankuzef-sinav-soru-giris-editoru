## Evaluation Guidelines & Item-Writing Criteria for Examination Pools

### 1. Structure, Timing, and Pacing

- **Item Count & Duration:** Each examination paper must consistently contain exactly 20 items.
- **Option Formatting:** Every item must feature exactly 5 multiple-choice options, formatted alphabetically from A through E.
- **Time Allocation:** The total testing duration per examination paper is strictly limited to 30 minutes.

### 2. Psychometric Balancing & Difficulty Distribution

- **Difficulty Allocation:** To ensure standardized evaluation, item difficulty within each 20-question test form must conform to the following distribution:
  - **Easy Items:** 2 questions
  - **Medium Items:** 16 questions
  - **Difficult Items:** 2 questions
- **Curricular Balance:** Questions must be proportionally distributed across all core learning units to ensure comprehensive topic coverage.

### 3. Option and Distractor Constraints

- **Absolute Option Restrictions:** The utilization of inclusive or exclusive collective options, such as "All of the above" or "None of the above", is **strictly prohibited**.
- **Plausibility & Validity:** Plausible distractors must be designed to capture legitimate misconceptions; ambiguous phrasing or deliberately deceptive traps are **strictly prohibited**.
- **Definitive Assessment:** Each multiple-choice item must feature exactly one clearly identifiable, scientifically verifiable correct answer.
- **Item Independence:** Test items and their respective options must remain completely independent; no question or option block may provide clues or cross-reference answers to other items within the exam paper.

### 4. Item Stem & Language Formatting Rules

- **Formatting Negative Phrasing:** When a negative qualifier (e.g., *not, except, false*) is required in the question stem, the negative term must be formatted using both **bold and underlined** styling to prevent student oversight.
- **Clarity & Precision:** Question stems must be articulated using precise, unambiguous, and concise academic language.
- **Content Validity:** All assessment items must align strictly with the approved course syllabus and instructional content; testing on out-of-scope material is forbidden.

### 5. Exam Pool Duplication & Security Mappings

- **Pool Isolation (Final vs. Resit):** Because the Final Examination and the Resit Examination share identical modular topics and learning outcomes, item-writers must develop 20 entirely distinct, non-overlapping questions for each pool to maintain exam security while adhering strictly to the same core scope.
